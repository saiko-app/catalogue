# Catalogue attribution

No data files have been released yet. This index records source credit and the intended licence boundaries. Each release must include its exact source versions, retrieval dates, hashes, changes, omissions and applicable notices. Mention here is not a claim that a source already ships.

## PsychonautWiki

Structured API data is by **PsychonautWiki contributors**, under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Wiki draft data is by **PsychonautWiki contributors**, under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt), except material that retains other upstream rights. See the [API](https://api.psychonautwiki.org/) and [copyright page](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Copyrights).

The prepared structured snapshot was retrieved on 28 October 2026; the prepared draft snapshot on 29 October 2026. These are candidate input dates, not published release versions. Structured API claims have unknown matched wiki revisions; draft claims retain their matched article revisions where known.

Saiko selects structured names, classes, dose and duration ranges; normalizes route labels, time units and phases; assigns stable identifiers; records review states; and withholds ambiguous, inapplicable, conflicting or withdrawn values from display. Forms and populations that the source does not establish remain unspecified. Source links and field locators stay with claims. No article images, experience reports or media are implied by this scope. Share-alike applies to the draft adaptations, not automatically to unrelated datasets.

Unresolved Erowid-attributed draft ranges are excluded. TripSit-attributed material retains TripSit's own terms and must not be described as BY-SA-only. Identity and scientific holds remain distinct from permission to redistribute.

## TripSit

Drug information and combination data are by **TripSit**, from the [TripSit drugs repository](https://github.com/TripSit/drugs). The prepared input uses revision `83670cb6bc8863615fcb9be081c87f061b42dea4`, retrieved on 28 October 2026. Releases will pin their actual revision and hashes.

Credit **TripSit** and link to **[TripSit](https://combo.tripsit.me/)** wherever the data is shown. The data is provided under [TripSit's non-commercial usage terms](https://wiki.tripsit.me/wiki/Drug_combinations#Use_.26_Attribution), recorded locally as [LicenseRef-TripSit-NonCommercial](LICENSES/LicenseRef-TripSit-NonCommercial.md). It is not GPL or Creative Commons data.

**This information is only a quick overview and reference. Users must perform individual research before making a decision.**

TripSit is included in the planned catalogue releases as separately licensed, removable data. Saiko reformats packaging and adds an identity index. Original drug and combination fields are preserved. Separate selected range projections normalize route names, time units and phase names and retain notes, unknown applicability and unreviewed states. Ambiguous or inapplicable values remain withheld. These changes must be listed with each actual artifact. No chart images or logos are included.

Commercial redistributors must omit the TripSit files unless they obtain applicable permission. Donations require a separate terms check. No use by another app is treated as a licence grant.
